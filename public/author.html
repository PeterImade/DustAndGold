About the author

Otito Nosike

Otito Nosike grew up in Lagos, Nigeria, where a lifelong fascination with literature first took root. From his secondary school years, he immersed himself in literary and debating societies, a passion he carried into the university and into the life he continues to build today.

He holds a Bachelor of Arts degree in History and International Relations from Paul University, Awka, and a Master’s degree in International Law and Diplomacy from the University of Lagos.

An insatiable reader with enduring interests in philosophy, religion, psychology, history, and the human condition, Otito is drawn to questions that resist easy answers and to the stories that shape the way people understand themselves and the world around them. He is the founder of Zeya, a writing startup committed to helping people write with greater depth and authenticity, and the creator of The Writing Forge, an online writing mentorship programme.

His writing lingers with ideas and emotions, allowing them to unfold before arriving at the events themselves.

It is marked by psychological depth, philosophical reflection, and a stubborn commitment to understanding the hidden forces that shape human lives.

On Becoming: Dust & Gold is his first book.
